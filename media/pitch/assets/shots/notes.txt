What-IF pitch screenshots: capture notes (2026-10-17)

Everything in the list was captured; nothing is missing.
Setup: Playwright Chromium 1.56.1, viewport 1440x900 at DPR 2 (phones: 390x844 at DPR 3, mobile and touch),
light colour scheme, reduced motion, en-GB locale, Europe/Berlin time zone. Served with
node scripts/dev.mjs --no-build --port 3340 after npm run build. No product code was changed.
The capture script is outside the repo, in the session scratchpad (capture/capture.mjs).

How the crops were made
- Each crop is the element's own box plus 16 px of padding (20 px for the two break-even blocks).
- While a crop is taken, the sticky header and step bar are hidden. Neighbouring elements wholly
  outside the crop are hidden too (visibility only, so nothing moves), so no other card shows at
  the edges.
- slider-00 ... slider-23: 24 positions (0 to 23 extra lunch customers a day). The owner's guess is
  slider-08. The clip box is the same in every frame (1760x1934). The readout and the "Why this
  changed" box were held at their tallest height, so the card is the same size in every frame.

Choices worth knowing
- report-chips-en: the report has no separate legend element. The "What the labels mean" link in
  step 2 points to the "Who did what" card (report-whodid-en). So this crop shows the step 2 line
  "Each number wears a label…" and the labelled groups below it. The landing hero (landing-hero-en)
  also has the four-chip legend: Your number / A guess / Calculated / Imagined.
- report-short-en and both break-even shots were captured after answering the step 4 guess
  (8 for the lunch deal, 5 for the price demo). Before a guess, the break-even tile reads
  "? guess first" (see report-predict-en).
- try-question-de and try-phone-de: "ca. 40" was typed, the German for "about 40". Both read as 40 a day.
- connect-en and connect-beta-en: the server was restarted on 3340 with
  WHATIF_BETA_CODES=BERLIN-DEMO, a local env setting only, so the code shows "Code accepted" and the
  link includes ?code=BERLIN-DEMO. The link reads http://localhost:3340/api/mcp?code=BERLIN-DEMO,
  because no public domain is configured. Retake this on the live https site, or cover the host in
  the edit, before it goes out.
- report-top-de (loaded with ?lang=de) and report-lang-switch-de (switched in the page) show the same view.
- AI set: report-top-ai-en matches report-top-en above the fold, because it has the same demo question
  and ribbon. report-rehearsal-ai-cards-en has the same 6 personas as the demo. The AI difference
  shows in the who-did-what card, caveat, grid, funnel, feed, interview and questions shots.
  report-rehearsal-ai-questions-en is tall (1760x3764) because it holds 23 questions for the owner.
  The three rows between "Check these with real customers" and "In their own words" were folded
  to fit it.

Things noticed in the product itself (left as they are)
- Landing at 1440 px wide: the header's "Try with your numbers" button wraps onto a second line.
- Landing method, step 4 mini chart: the label "break-even: 11 a day" is cut to "break-even: 11 a".
- Step 6 "Which guess matters most": the €642 value label sticks out past the inner box border.
- Rule-based demo interview (report-interview-en): answers repeat in pairs (Chiara = Paweł,
  Noor = Greta, Murat = Jan). The AI interview (report-rehearsal-ai-interview-en) has six different
  answers, so it reads better in a pitch.
- "In their own words" panel: the h4 headings sit flush against the panel's left edge.
- /connect: the see-through sticky header shows faint ghost text from the content scrolled under it.
